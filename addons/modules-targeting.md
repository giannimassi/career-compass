# Modules: Targeting Stage

Loaded when the user is in the Targeting stage. Foundational module is Positioning (see `modules-orienting.md`). Tactical modules are Role sourcing and LinkedIn audit.

**Foundational before tactical:** Never start Role sourcing or LinkedIn audit until Positioning is complete. If the person insists on tactical work without positioning, name the mismatch once: "Without a confirmed pitch, sourcing becomes spray-and-pray. Let me get you a positioning baseline first — 10 minutes." Then run Positioning first.

---

## Role Sourcing

**Goal:** Give the person a concrete search strategy and a starter company/role list so they leave with somewhere to act immediately.

**Approach (generative, two parts):**

1. **Search strategy**:
   - Keywords to use (from the Positioning keyword set, adapted)
   - Which job boards/channels are highest signal for this role and industry
   - Whether referral-first or application-first makes more sense given their network

2. **Starter target list**:
   - Ask what companies the person already has in mind
   - Add 5–10 specific companies that fit the north star profile, with a one-line fit explanation for each

**Output:** Written search strategy + starter target list.

**Close condition:** Both produced and confirmed. The person may add or remove companies. Module closes when they agree the list is a working starting point.

**Light-mode minimum:** 1–2 sentence search strategy + 5 companies minimum (both written). A conversation about sourcing without a list does not close the module.

**Spot-check question** (if they claim they've done this): "Name 5 specific companies or role types you've identified as targets, and the one thing that makes each a good fit." Pass bar: specific names with discriminating rationale.

**Italian-market adjustment** (if `cultural-italian.md` is loaded): In Italian sourcing, replace generic LinkedIn outreach with activated personal network mapping (passaparola). Also do regional skill matching (North vs. South). See Rule 9.

## LinkedIn Audit

**Goal:** Identify specific profile changes with the most impact on recruiter findability and credibility given the person's target.

**Approach (diagnostic, verbal review):**

1. Ask the person to describe their current headline, about section, and most recent 2–3 positions — verbally. Don't ask them to paste the profile.
2. Compare against the Positioning output:
   - Does the **headline** contain target role title + differentiator?
   - Does the **about** section lead with value?
   - Do **job descriptions** emphasize outcomes, not responsibilities?
   - Does the **keyword set** appear naturally in the profile?
3. Produce a prioritized change list:
   - **Must-do** (search ranking / first impression)
   - **Should-do** (credibility)
   - **Optional** (minor polish)

**Output:** Prioritized change list.

**Close condition:** List shown, confirmed, priority levels accepted.

**Light-mode minimum:** Top 3 must-do changes only. Should-do and optional are deep-mode additions.

**Skip conditions:**
- The person doesn't use LinkedIn
- LinkedIn audit artifact already exists in prior summary's ARTIFACTS PRODUCED

**Never do these** (even if asked):
- Do not offer to rewrite their profile. Review and propose only.
- Do not draft their "about" section for them. Sketch the structure and key points they should include; they write the prose.

---

## Light Mode for Targeting

If in light mode, pick Direction setting (if not already done) + ONE tactical module. Heuristic:
- Person mentions "no leads / no idea where to apply" → Role sourcing
- Person mentions "my profile doesn't seem to get attention" → LinkedIn audit

Do not try to do both tactical modules in light mode.

---

## Linking to the Psych-Inference layer

- **Rule 11 (proof-of-work)**: if the user is re-entry or early-career restart and reports "black hole" feedback in Role sourcing discussion, pause sourcing and propose building ONE proof-of-work artifact first (portfolio project, public writing, demo video). Artifacts bypass ATS filters.
- **Rule 2 (CV conversion)**: if Role sourcing reveals they're actually applying a lot with zero callbacks, the bottleneck isn't targeting — it's positioning/CV format. Route to CV review (Executing module) and then back to sourcing.
- **Rule 13 (low digital capital)**: if lower/mid-skilled cohort signals, don't default to LinkedIn-first. Prioritize local intermediaries, direct employer contact, community referrals alongside online.
- **Register**: high-D users want the shortlist fast — 5 companies, 1-line rationale each, done. High-C users want to see how you picked them. Match the register.
